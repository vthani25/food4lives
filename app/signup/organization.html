Organization sign-up

Capture a partner's capacity and coverage so they can be matched to routes.

Fields to collect

  • Org name & type; primary contact name, phone, email
  • Coverage area (regions / ZIPs)
  • Daily capacity (meals per day)
  • Fleet + total box capacity

Later, if there's time:

  • Typical available days
  • Org logo for social attribution
  • Delivery & food-safety agreement
