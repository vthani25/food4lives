Volunteer sign-up

Collect exactly what the Admin needs to assign this person to the right task.

Fields to collect

  • Name, phone, email
  • Home base / ZIP
  • Skills: Cook / Pack / Drive / Pickup
  • Vehicle + box capacity
  • Availability: days + AM/PM windows
  • Emergency contact

Later, if there's time:

  • Preferred kitchen
  • "Can host prep at home" flag
  • Recurring temple-run commitment

Actions the user can take

  • Submit and create account, then land in the Volunteer Portal
